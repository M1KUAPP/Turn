## Overview

At RevenueCat, we're all about supporting developers to build and monetize
amazing apps. After the overwhelming success of last year's Shipaton, we're
thrilled to announce the return of our global hackathon — and this year, we're
going bigger than ever.

This August and September, we're inviting builders from all corners of the globe
to ship new apps and compete for over $700,000 in cash prizes and truly unique
rewards — over $1 million worth of prizes in total.

## The challenge

- Ship a brand-new app to the App Store, Google Play Store, or — new this year —
  the Samsung Galaxy Store, between August 1st and September 30th, 2026.
- Integrate the RevenueCat SDK to power at least one in-app purchase or serve
  ads through RevenueCat Ads.

Our goal is to give you that final push to ship that project you've had in the
back of your mind but never took the time to get started.

_Note: Updates to previously released apps won't qualify - it's all about
bringing something new to life! You can brainstorm, build, and post before
August 1st, but your app must be released for the first time during the Shipaton
window._

## Bigger and better

Last year's Shipaton blew us away. Hundreds of new apps launched, thousands of
\#BuildInPublic posts went out, communities from Tokyo to Seoul to London hosted

Inspired by your amazing contributions, we've upped the ante for 2026:

## Categories

- **Grand Prize:** Goes to the app that gains the most user traction and growth
  momentum during the event. We want to hear about what you've done post-release
  to push your app growth to the next level.
- **\[NEW\] Next Gen Award:** A student-only category for the best app submitted
  by active students — high school, university, bootcamp, campus dev club,
  anyone learning the craft. Judged on a video submission and open-source code,
  so no paid Apple or Google developer account is required. Open to students 13
  and up. If you're under the age of majority where you live, a parent or
  guardian just needs to consent and agree to the rules on your behalf. Learn
  more on the [Next Gen page](https://www.shipaton.com/next-gen) .
- **\[NEW\] Catvertising Award:** Celebrates the most creative and effective use
  of RevenueCat Ads as a monetization method. We're looking for clever
  placements, smart integration with the rest of the revenue stack, and an
  experience users don't hate.
- **\[NEW\] Best Game Award:** For the best mobile game shipped during the
  event. We're looking for great gameplay, art direction, and a monetization fit
  that suits the genre.
- **#BuildInPublic Award:** For developers who share the most interesting
  development journey on social media. We're looking for compelling lessons
  learned or clever ideas that came from engaging with the community.
- **HAMM Award (Help Apps Make Money):** RevenueCat exists to "Help Apps Make
  More Money". This award goes to the project that designs the most robust and
  creative monetization strategy.
- **RevenueCat Design Award:** For the app that best represents the craft of app
  development — regardless of business potential. We're looking for innovative
  ideas and/or beautiful app design and animations.
- **RevenueCat Peace Prize:** Awarded to the project that provides the greatest
  social good. We're looking for apps with big benefits to communities or
  society at large.
- **Conflict of Interest Award:** A special category for RevenueCat employees
  and sponsors who couldn't resist joining the fun. No cash prizes, but a shot
  at glory.

- **Influencer Award — Productivity: Christopher Lawley**

  For a polished productivity app that gives Apple power users one fast, focused
  place to save and retrieve reusable text snippets, documents, files, and
  images. Judges will consider product focus, speed, organization, intelligent
  features, and the quality of the overall design.
- **Influencer Award — Nutrition & Healthy Eating: Abbey’s Kitchen**

  For a flexible nutrition app inspired by Abbey’s Hunger Crushing Combo
  framework. Judges are looking for a compassionate, practical experience that
  helps users create more satisfying meals in real-life situations without
  calorie counting, macro tracking, or restrictive meal plans.
- **Influencer Award — Yoga & Fitness: Simone Sharice**

  For a personalized wellness companion that answers, “What should I do today?”
  Judges will consider how effectively the app turns movement, Pilates,
  recovery, and other wellness needs into a clear, achievable daily plan without
  creating more information overload.
- **Influencer Award — Career Coaching: Leadership Heather**

  For an app that helps new managers practise difficult workplace conversations
  before the stakes are real. Judges are looking for realistic scenarios, useful
  feedback, confidence-building experiences, and active practice with situations
  such as feedback, boundaries, and saying no.
- **Influencer Award — Gaming: Mr Lewis Blogs Gaming**

For a gaming bucket-list app that makes it easy to save games at the moment of
discovery. Judges will consider how effectively users can organize, complete,
rate, and share games, along with whether managing a backlog feels enjoyable
rather than like another chore.

- **Ship Kotlin Everywhere — JetBrains**

  Recognizes a creative, polished app built for both iOS and Android using
  Kotlin Multiplatform, Compose Multiplatform, or both. Judges will consider
  cross-platform quality, consistency, and effective use of Kotlin. Optional
  consideration may be given to useful build content and contributions to the
  Kotlin Multiplatform community.
- **Most Viral App — Noise**

  Awarded to the app that captures the most attention in its market through
  high-quality, scalable content that converts into downloads. Judges will
  consider virality, whether the creative formats can be repeated at scale, and
  how clearly the content communicates the app’s value and addresses users’
  needs.
- **Best App for Galaxy — Samsung**

  Recognizes a high-quality app published on the Galaxy Store and thoughtfully
  optimized for Samsung Galaxy users. Judges will consider the overall app
  experience and Galaxy-specific optimization, including foldable-device
  support, Samsung features, and device performance. Galaxy Store exclusivity
  may receive bonus consideration but is not required.
- **Replit’s Idea to Income**

  Recognizes a project that uses Replit to turn a new idea into a live,
  monetizing mobile app. Judges will primarily consider consistent
  week-over-week growth in revenue and transactions, supported by paying-user
  traction, product craft, visual design, and meaningful community engagement.
- **Keep Them Coming Back Award — OneSignal**

  Recognizes an app that uses OneSignal thoughtfully to bring users back. Judges
  will consider the quality and stability of the implementation, the value it
  adds to the user experience, and how creatively the app uses campaigns, push
  notifications, Journeys, or advanced messaging capabilities.
- **The Growth Loop Award — Layers**

  Recognizes a team that treats growth as a process of testing, measuring,
  learning, and improving. Judges will consider the clarity of the audience and
  growth hypothesis, how Layers was used to run and observe a focused
  experiment, whether the SDK was installed correctly, and what the team learned
  or would try next.
- **Funnel Vision Award — Stripe**

Recognizes a project that launches a web funnel using RevenueCat Funnels and
Stripe. Judges will consider the qualifying web payment volume processed during
the measurement period and whether the funnel is thoughtfully designed,
functional, and effectively implemented.

## Requirements

### What to build

Entrants must create a working software application that uses the RevenueCat SDK
to power at least one in-app or web purchase (each a "Project"). Apps must be
built for iOS, iPadOS, macOS, or Android. The first public version of your app
must be released between **August 1 and September 30, 2026**.

**Students competing for the Next Gen Award** follow a different, lower-friction
path: submit a video and source code instead of a published store listing. No
paid Apple or Google developer account required.

Students under the age of majority where they reside may enter the Next Gen
Award (minimum age 13) with parent or guardian consent. See Section 3 of the
official rules.

### What to submit on Devpost

Every submission must include:

- A **text description** of your app's features and functionality.
- A **demo video** of your project. The video:

- Should show the app running on the device it was built for.
    - Must be no longer than 2 minutes of essential footage — **judges are not
      required to watch beyond two minutes.**
    - Must be uploaded to and publicly visible on YouTube or Vimeo, with the
      link provided on this form.
    - Must not include third-party trademarks or copyrighted music/material
      unless you have permission to use it.
- A **URL to your fully published app** on the App Store or Google Play or
  Samsung Galaxy Store.
- A **1024×1024 app icon**.
- At least **one screenshot** of the app at **1179px × 2556px** with **no device
  frame**.
- Either a **free trial** in your app, **or** a **promo code** so judges can
  unlock the in-app purchase and test all premium features.

## Judging criteria

- **Grand Prize**: Honors the app that gains the most user traction and growth
  momentum during the event. Judges want to hear about what you've done
  post-release to push your app growth to the next level. See official rules for
  full judging criteria.

- **Best Game Award**: Open category for the best mobile game shipped during the
  event. We're looking for great gameplay, art direction, and a monetization fit
  that suits the genre. See official rules for full judging criteria.

- **RevenueCat Peace Prize**: Awarded to the project that provides the greatest
  social good. We're looking for apps with big benefits to communities or
  society at large.

- **RevenueCat Design Award**: For the app that best represents the craft of app
  development, separate from viability as a business. We're looking for
  innovative ideas and/or beautiful app design and animations.

- **Catvertising Award**: Celebrates the most creative and effective use of ads
  as a monetization method. We're looking for clever placements, smart
  integration with the rest of the revenue stack, and an experience users don't
  hate.

- **Next Gen Award**: A student-only category for the best app submitted by
  active students with a .edu (or equivalent) email address. Judged on a video
  submission and open-source code — no App Store or Google Play release
  required.

- **#BuildInPublic Award**: For developers who shared the most interesting
  development journey on social media. We're looking for the most compelling
  lessons learned, or ideas incorporated from feedback from the community.

- **HAMM Award**: Recognizes the app that makes the smartest use of RevenueCat
  to drive real revenue.

- **Conflict of Interest Award**: Awarded to the best overall app submission
  from employees of RevenueCat or any Shipaton sponsors.

- **Influencer Award — Productivity: Christopher Lawley**: Build a polished app
  that helps Apple power users quickly save, organize, and retrieve reusable
  text, files, documents, and images.

- **Influencer Award — Nutrition & Healthy Eating: Abbey’s Kitchen**: Create a
  flexible nutrition app that helps people make meals more satisfying without
  calorie counting, macro tracking, or restrictive meal plans.

- **Influencer Award — Yoga & Fitness: Simone Sharice**: Create a personalized
  wellness companion that turns movement, Pilates, and recovery needs into a
  clear and achievable plan for today.

- **Influencer Award — Gaming: Mr Lewis Blogs Gaming**: Build a gaming bucket
  list where players can easily save, organize, complete, rate, and share the
  games they want to play.

- **Influencer Award — Career Coaching: Leadership Heather**: Help new managers
  practise difficult conversations, receive useful feedback, and build
  confidence before handling the real situation.

- **Replit’s Idea to Income**: Use Replit to turn a new idea into a live,
  monetizing mobile app with growing revenue, returning customers, strong
  design, and real community interest.

- **Best App for Galaxy — Samsung**: Publish a high-quality app on the Galaxy
  Store and make it shine on Samsung devices through thoughtful optimization,
  features, and foldable support.

- **Most Viral App — Noise**: Use Noise to create engaging, repeatable content
  that captures attention, communicates your app’s value, and converts viewers
  into users.

- **Ship Kotlin Everywhere — JetBrains**: Build a polished app for iOS and
  Android with Kotlin Multiplatform or Compose Multiplatform, delivering a
  consistent, high-quality experience on both platforms.

- **Keep Them Coming Back Award — OneSignal**: Use OneSignal to create
  thoughtful notifications, campaigns, or Journeys that improve the experience
  and give users a meaningful reason to return.

- **The Growth Loop Award — Layers**: Use Layers to test a focused growth idea,
  measure the response, explain what you learned, and show how those insights
  will shape your next experiment.

- **Funnel Vision Award — Stripe**: Launch a polished web payment funnel with
  RevenueCat Funnels and Stripe that works reliably, converts users, and
  processes real payment volume.
