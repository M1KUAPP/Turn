## Three reasons to bring your campus.

Most student hackathons end with a demo video and a repo nobody opens again.
Shipaton ends with a shipped app. Active students aged 13 and older get their
own prize category, their own scoreboard, and a reason to bring the whole campus
along. Students under the age of majority where they live need consent from a
parent or legal guardian.

### Your own prize category

The Next Gen Award is open to active students aged 13 and older. Submit a video
and source code; no store listing or paid developer account is required.

### A scoreboard for your school

The Campus Leaderboard ranks schools by student applicants today and by
submissions from September, so getting classmates on board moves your campus up.

### A party for the campus that ships

The school with the most submissions wins a Learning Party: a fully sponsored
event on or near campus with food, speakers, swag, and RevenueCat engineers.

Next Gen Award

## A lower-friction way into the competition.

Students should not have to buy an Apple or Google developer account just to
compete. The Next Gen Award lets active students aged 13 and older enter with a
video and their source code. Students under the age of majority where they live
need consent from a parent or legal guardian and may compete only for Next Gen.

[See the Next Gen Award](https://www.shipaton.com/next-gen)
[Register for Shipaton](https://www.shipaton.com/go/devpost?to=%2F)

Built for first-time shippers.

High school, university, bootcamp, or another academic program: if you are at
least 13 and learning to build, this category is for you.

Campus Leaderboard

## See where your school ranks.

The leaderboard tracks student participation by campus and updates as new
builders register. It currently ranks schools by student applicants; in
September it switches to ranking by submissions. Search for your school, filter
by region, scan the global map, and rally classmates to climb the board before
submissions close.

[View the Campus Leaderboard](https://www.shipaton.com/campus-leaderboard)
[Host a campus event](https://www.shipaton.com/host)

Campus pride, now with a scoreboard.

Every classmate you get registered counts toward your school's standing.

The grand prize for campuses

## What is a learning party?

The school with the most Shipaton submissions wins a Learning Party — a
celebration hosted on or near campus and sponsored entirely by RevenueCat. It is
part party, part workshop: the people who build subscription apps for a living
come to your school and help your students do the same.

[Check the standings](https://www.shipaton.com/campus-leaderboard)
[Apply to host an event](https://www.shipaton.com/host)

Fully sponsored by RevenueCat, hosted on or near the winning campus.

Food and drinks for every student who shows up — whether or not they submitted
to Shipaton.

Talks from speakers and developer influencers in the mobile community.

Shipaton swag for students.

Live office hours with RevenueCat engineers on how to monetize an app and build
a business around it.
