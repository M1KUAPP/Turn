## What are build-in-public partners?

Build-in-Public partners are communities and platforms where Shipaton
participants can share what they’re building throughout the hackathon.

### Use these spaces to:

- Share your app idea
- Post updates and demos
- Ask for feedback
- Find teammates or collaborators
- Get help with technical or business blockers
- Learn how to launch and promote
- Tell the story behind your build

Note: You don’t need to post everywhere. Choose the communities that feel most
relevant to your app, your audience, and the kind of feedback you want.

## Official build-in-public partners

### r/androiddev

Get feedback from Android developers

r/androiddev is a community for Android developers to discuss what they’re
building, ask technical questions, and connect with other Android builders.

- **How to engage:** Subscribe to the subreddit and post your progress, join
  check-in threads, ask for technical feedback, and bring your questions about
  your android or KMP stack.

Keep an eye out for an upcoming AMA with Jaewoong from RevenueCat. Bring
questions about your Shipaton project, RevenueCat implementation, app
monetization, or technical blockers.

- **Good for:** Native Android apps, Kotlin Multiplatform, technical feedback,
  architecture questions, community discussion.

[Join the discussion onr/androiddev](https://www.reddit.com/r/androiddev)

### HackerNoon

Tell the story behind what you’re building

HackerNoon is where developers, founders, and startup teams publish stories
about what they’re building, learning, launching, and figuring out along the
way.

- **How to engage:** Publish articles about your Shipaton journey using the
  \#shipaton tag. Share what you’re building, what you’re learning, and what you
  discover along the way.

Share your Shipaton story on HackerNoon to compete for an additional $2,500
prize pool for outstanding Build-in-Public stories.

- **Good for:** Long-form Build-in-Public updates, founder stories, technical
  writeups, launch reflections, lessons learned

[Share your story onHackerNoon](http://www.hackernoon.com/)

### Product Hunt

Prepare your app for launch

Product Hunt helps makers launch new products, collect feedback, and get
discovered by a community of early adopters, founders, and builders.

- **How to engage:** Use Product Hunt’s launch guide to prepare your Shipaton
  app for launch. Think through your positioning, assets, launch plan, and how
  you’ll rally support when your app is ready.

Check out the
[Product Hunt Shipaton Launch Guide](https://www.notion.so/teamhome1431/Ship-a-ton-Product-Hunt-Launch-Guide-38e2e1256c9e80ddb025c5012ec1e45a?source=copy_link)
for tips on preparing your app for launch and making the most of your debut.

- **Good for:** Launch planning, positioning, product feedback, getting your app
  in front of early users.

[Read theProduct Hunt launch guide](https://www.notion.so/teamhome1431/Ship-a-ton-Product-Hunt-Launch-Guide-38e2e1256c9e80ddb025c5012ec1e45a?source=copy_link)

### r/AppBusiness

Talk growth, monetization, and the business of apps

r/AppBusiness is a community for app founders and builders focused on the
business side of apps: growth, monetization, positioning, launch strategy, and
feedback.

- **How to engage:** Subscribe to the subreddit and Join regular Shipaton
  check-in threads, share what you’re building, ask for feedback, and look for
  collaborators.

Join dedicated check-in threads and use the Shipaton flair when posting about
your app!

- **Good for:** App business questions, growth and monetization feedback, launch
  strategy

[Join the discussion onr/AppBusiness](http://www.reddit.com/r/appbusiness)
