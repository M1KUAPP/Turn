## What judges are looking for

The Catvertising Award celebrates the most creative and effective use of
RevenueCat Ads as a monetization method. Judges are looking for clever
placements, smart integration with the rest of the revenue stack, and an
experience users do not hate.

The strongest submissions treat ads as part of the product strategy, not a
bolt-on.

## What to include

- A description of how the app uses RevenueCat Ads.
- Where ads appear and why those placements make sense.
- How ads work alongside subscriptions, purchases, trials, or other
  monetization.
- Any early signal that the ad experience performs well without harming the user
  experience.
