## About the campus leaderboard

Right now these standings rank schools by **student applicants** — the builders
who have registered for Shipaton so far. In September, the leaderboard switches
to ranking schools by **submissions**, and the school that finishes with the
most submissions wins a Learning Party with food, speakers, swag, and RevenueCat
engineers.
