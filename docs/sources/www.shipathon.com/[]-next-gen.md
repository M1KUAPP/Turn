## A lower-friction path into Shipaton.

The Next Gen Award is the student category for Shipaton 2026. Active students
aged 13 and older can build, learn, and show what they can make by submitting a
video and source code instead of an app store listing. Students under the age of
majority where they live need a parent or legal guardian to consent and agree to
the official rules on their behalf.

### Built for students

The Next Gen Award gives active students aged 13 and older their own prize lane,
whether they are in high school, university, bootcamp, or another academic
program.

### No store submission required

Students can submit a video and source code, so they do not need to buy an Apple
or Google developer account just to participate.

### Open from age 13

Students under the age of majority where they live can enter with consent from a
parent or legal guardian. Teams that include a minor may compete only for Next
Gen.

For minor entrants

### Ask a parent or guardian to confirm your entry.

If you are under the age of majority where you live, your parent or legal
guardian must review the official rules and complete the consent form. Teams
that include a minor may compete only for the Next Gen Award.

## Check your academic email.

The Next Gen Award is for active students aged 13 and older in high school,
college, university, bootcamp, or another academic program. Students under the
age of majority where they live need consent from a parent or legal guardian.
You will also need a student or academic email on Devpost. This check uses
JetBrains/swot and confirms the email domain only. Review the
[official rules](https://www.shipaton.com/go/devpost?to=%2Frules) for every
eligibility requirement.

Email domain checks use [JetBrains/swot](https://github.com/jetbrains/swot) .

## Turn Shipaton into a campus hackathon.

Approved campus events can receive Shipaton swag to give out to student
