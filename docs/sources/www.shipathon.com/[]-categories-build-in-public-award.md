## What judges are looking for

The **#BuildInPublic Award** is for developers who shared an interesting
development journey on social media or in online communities. Judges are looking
for compelling lessons learned, community feedback, and ways the public process
improved the app.

Audience size does not matter. The quality and usefulness of the journey does.

## What to include

- Links to relevant social accounts or specific posts you want judges to review.
- A short explanation of how building in public helped the app.
- Examples of feedback, motivation, or ideas that came from sharing publicly.
- Posts tagged `#Shipaton` and optionally `#BuildInPublic` where appropriate.

## Judging focus

Judges look at how creatively you shared the process, how people responded, and
what you learned. They also consider whether public feedback led to changes that
made the app better.

The official Devpost rules are the source of truth for eligibility, deadlines,
and legal requirements.
