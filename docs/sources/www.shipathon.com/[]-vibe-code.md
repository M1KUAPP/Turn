## Not sure you're ready? we bet you are

### I don't have an idea

That's fine. Start with a need you or someone close to you has that nobody's
quite solved yet, or check reviews of similar apps to find something people wish
existed. Still stuck? Watch our livestream on coming up with an app idea.

[Watch the idea livestreams](https://www.shipaton.com/livestreams) ### I've
never touched App Store Connect

Most vibe coders haven't either. That's exactly what our resources and community
are for, so you don't have to figure out anything on your own.

### I'll run out of time

You've got until September 30, and most vibe-coded apps go from idea to store in
days. Build your 1.0 fast, then keep improving it right up to the deadline.

### I don't know how to make money from my app

Your app only needs one working purchase or ad. Ask your AI tool what to charge
for, then have it wire up RevenueCat to power the purchase. Judges can test it
with a free trial or a promo code you provide.

### Isn't this just another AI app nobody notices

Probably not, if you ship something specific enough to be useful to someone. A
small, single-purpose app that solves one thing can be just what users need (and
what wins the prize).
