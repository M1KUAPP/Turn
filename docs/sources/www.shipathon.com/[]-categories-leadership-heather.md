## The career coaching challenge

Heather's audience includes first-time and newly promoted managers in their late
20s and 30s, often managing people who used to be their peers. They know a
difficult conversation is coming, rehearse it over and over, and still struggle
when the moment arrives.

Build somewhere they can practise feedback, boundaries, saying no, and other
high-stakes conversations before the stakes are real. The idea is not a script
generator. Think of it as a **flight simulator for hard conversations**: a safe
environment for trying, learning, adjusting, and building confidence.

## What judges are looking for

Judges are looking for a solution that:

- Lets new managers actively practise rather than passively read advice.
- Creates realistic, useful scenarios around feedback, boundaries, and saying
  no.
- Gives feedback that helps the user improve their approach and confidence.
- Supports authentic leadership rather than prescribing one perfect script.
- Feels safe enough to experiment in and practical enough to use before a real
  conversation.

## About leadership heather

Heather brings a fresh approach to modern leadership. After moving directly from
university into managing large teams at Harrods and FTSE 100 companies, she
learned how to engage and inspire Gen Z professionals. Now a content creator and
small-business owner, she shares proven tools, strategies, and insights that
help a growing community of professionals lead successfully.

## Heather's audience

Heather's typical audience member is a professional woman in her 30s or 40s who
is navigating leadership for the first time. She may be managing a team at a
corporate or growing company and wants practical tools and real strategies to
lead confidently without losing her authentic voice. Heather's Instagram
audience is 75% female and 25% male, with the largest age groups being 35-44
(45%) and 25-34 (36%).

Her top audience countries are the United States (22%), the United Kingdom
(22%), India (7%), Australia (6%), and Canada (4%). The full age breakdown is
18-24 (1%), 25-34 (36%), 35-44 (45%), 45-64 (17%), and 65+ (1%).
