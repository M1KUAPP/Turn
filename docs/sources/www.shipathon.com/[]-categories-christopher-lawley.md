## The productivity challenge

Christopher's audience wants to get more from the Apple devices they already
own. They value productivity, automation, useful features, and intelligent
design, but they are missing a quick way to store and retrieve the content they
reuse most often.

Build an app that brings text snippets, images, files, and documents together in
one fast, focused place. It should work for things like canned email replies,
documents someone regularly shows to clients, and information used to fill out
forms.

The solution should be more purpose-built than a notes app without becoming a
full file manager. Most importantly, it should be thoughtfully designed so
people can pull up whatever they need with as little friction as possible.

## What judges are looking for

Judges are looking for a polished solution that:

- Handles text snippets, images, files, and documents in one coherent
  experience.
- Makes saving, organizing, finding, and reusing content quick and intuitive.
- Feels focused on this specific problem rather than behaving like a
  general-purpose notes app or file manager.
- Uses intelligent features and thoughtful interaction design to reduce
  friction.
- Demonstrates the care, speed, and utility that Apple power users expect.
