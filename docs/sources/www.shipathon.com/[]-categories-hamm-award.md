## What judges are looking for

The HAMM Award recognizes the app that makes the smartest use of RevenueCat to
drive real revenue. Judges are looking for a well-crafted paywall, thoughtful
pricing and packaging, strong conversion, and monetization that genuinely fits
the product.

This category is about making money in a way that feels intentional and
sustainable.

## What to include

- A description of your monetization strategy.
- Your paywall, pricing, packaging, and trial approach.
- Any conversion, revenue, retention, or purchase data you can share.
- How RevenueCat helped you build, test, or manage the monetization flow.
