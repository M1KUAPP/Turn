## 21 categories: one big reason to ship.

From building in public to design, games, and growth — these prizes recognize
creativity, impact, and the hustle that ships.

- [Grand Prize](https://www.shipaton.com/categories/grand-prize) : For the app
  with the strongest traction and growth momentum.
- [BuildInPublic Award](https://www.shipaton.com/categories/build-in-public-award)
  : For the most compelling build-in-public journey.
- [Best Game Award](https://www.shipaton.com/categories/best-game-award) : For
  the best mobile game shipped during Shipaton.
- [RevenueCat Peace Prize](https://www.shipaton.com/categories/revenuecat-peace-prize)
  : For the app with the biggest positive impact.
- [RevenueCat Design Award](https://www.shipaton.com/categories/revenuecat-design-award)
  : For exceptional product craft, design, and animation.
- [Catvertising Award](https://www.shipaton.com/categories/catvertising-award) :
  Celebrates the most creative and effective use of ads as a monetization
  method.
- [Next Gen Award](https://www.shipaton.com/categories/next-gen-award) : For
  student builders submitting a video and open-source code.
- [HAMM Award (Help Apps Make Money)](https://www.shipaton.com/categories/hamm-award)
  : For the smartest use of RevenueCat to drive revenue.
- [Conflict of Interest Award](https://www.shipaton.com/categories/conflict-of-interest-award)
  : For the best eligible submission from RevenueCat or sponsor employees.

[Read full rules on Devpost](https://www.shipaton.com/go/devpost?to=%2Frules)

## Next gen gives student builders their own path to win.

This year we're making Shipaton more accessible than ever to students across the
world with the **Next Gen Award**: a prize category for active students aged 13
and older. Participants submit a video and source code, **removing the
requirement for a paid Apple or Google developer account**. Students under the
age of majority where they live need consent from a parent or legal guardian.

Schools, clubs, and university programs can host Shipaton IRL campus events to
bring students together, hand out Shipaton swag, and help classmates build
toward their submissions.

[Explore Next Gen](https://www.shipaton.com/next-gen)
[Host a campus event](https://www.shipaton.com/host)

## Shipaton IRL brings builders together around the world.

Shipaton is online, but it does not have to feel remote. Last year, builders
