## Presented by Samsung

Publish an app on the Galaxy Store during Shipaton 2026 and optimize it for
Galaxy users.

## Prizes

_Check [DevPost](https://www.shipaton.com/go/devpost?to=%2F) for official,
up-to-date prizes._

- **1st Place**
    - 3 weeks of featured editorial placement on the Galaxy Store's Apps and
      Discover tabs
    - Invitation to RevenueCat's App Growth Annual conference in New York City
    - Times Square billboard feature
    - Shippy trophy
    - Blog feature
    - Media spotlight on 9to5Mac and 9to5Google
- **2nd Place**
    - Blog feature
- **3rd Place**
    - Blog feature

## Requirements

Your app must be published on the Galaxy Store during Shipaton 2026 and comply
with the store's content guidelines.

## What judges are looking for

Entries follow the standard Shipaton judging criteria. Judges will also consider
Galaxy Store optimization, including foldable support, Samsung-specific
features, and thoughtful device optimization.

Galaxy Store exclusivity can earn bonus points, but it is not required.

## What to include

- A live app URL.
- A description of how you optimized the app for the Galaxy Store and Galaxy
  devices.
