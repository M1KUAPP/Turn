## Presented by Layers

Treat growth as something to test, measure, learn from, and improve. This award
is about the quality of the loop, not who has the biggest audience or the most
downloads.

## Requirements

Install and verify the Layers SDK in the app you submit.

## What judges are looking for

- **Audience and hypothesis:** Did you identify a specific audience and a clear
  reason they might care?
- **Layers-assisted growth loop:** Did you use Layers to define a focused
  message, channel or surface, experiment, and intended outcome?
- **SDK installation and observability:** Is the Layers SDK installed and
  verified so the loop can be observed and measured?
- **Learning and iteration:** Can you explain what happened, what you learned,
  and what you would try next?

## What to include

A few sentences for each item is enough:

- **Layers usage:** How you used Layers during Shipaton and confirmation that
  the SDK was installed.
- **Growth loop:** The audience, message, channel or surface, and experiment you
  focused on.
- **Learning and next step:** What the SDK signal or early response showed, what
  you learned, and what you would try next.
