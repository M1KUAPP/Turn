## Bring the next gen award to campus.

Students aged 13 and older can host a Shipaton hackathon night, club workshop,
or weekend sprint at their school so classmates can build for the Next Gen Award
without needing a store submission or paid developer account. Students under the
age of majority where they live need consent from a parent or legal guardian.

[Apply to host](https://forms.gle/5aaXPEs3CvMCYFVbA)
[Read about Next Gen](https://www.shipaton.com/next-gen)

### Student-first format

Make Shipaton easy for classmates who are learning mobile development, testing
new ideas, or building their first real app.

### School-friendly promotion

Rally CS clubs, entrepreneurship centers, design programs, and faculty around
one clear student prize category.

### No developer account blocker

The Next Gen Award does not require an app store submission, so students aged 13
and older can participate before paying for a developer account.
