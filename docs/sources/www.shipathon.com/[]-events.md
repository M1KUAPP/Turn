## Turn your campus into a launch pad for real apps

Shipaton gives schools a ready-made way to help students move beyond prototypes.
Campus hosts bring the competition into a familiar room where classmates can
find teammates, get unstuck, learn what it takes to publish, and connect their
work to a global app-building moment.

2026 local events

Shipaton is targeting more campus and community gatherings.

### Real shipping experience

Students launch, monetize, market, and compete with real apps instead of
stopping at a class project or demo repo.

### Built-in global momentum

Your event plugs into Shipaton promotion, creator collaborations, press, prizes,
and a worldwide builder calendar.
