## Requirements

Your app must be available on both iOS and Android and built with Kotlin
Multiplatform, Compose Multiplatform, or both.

## What judges are looking for

- **Cross-platform quality — required:** A creative, polished app built for
  multiple platforms. Consistency and quality matter more than the number of
  platforms supported.
- **Community interaction — optional:** Share the work in a way people enjoy,
  such as a useful blog post, devlog, article, video, or social update
  explaining how you built the app.
- **Giving back — optional:** Help the Kotlin Multiplatform ecosystem by
  publishing a library, contributing to a library you use, open-sourcing a
  reusable part of the app, or improving documentation, samples, or tooling.

## What to include

- Live links to the fully published app on both Apple's App Store and Google
  Play.
- A description of how you used Kotlin Multiplatform or Compose Multiplatform.
- Links to any public posts where you shared what you learned.
- A description of your contribution to the ecosystem, with links to any library
  or pull request.
