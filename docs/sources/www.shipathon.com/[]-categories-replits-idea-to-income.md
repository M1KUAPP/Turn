Replit and RevenueCat.

## Requirements

Build and publish a live app with Replit, then integrate RevenueCat using Replit
Agent.

## What judges are looking for

- **Growth momentum — primary:** Fast, consistent week-over-week growth in
  revenue and transactions. The trajectory matters more than the total.
- **Traction:** Gross revenue and paying-user count as supporting signals.
- **Craft:** A strong user experience and design that feels like a real product.
- **Social pull:** Community engagement and buzz around the app.

## What to include

- Three social posts showing different stages of the build journey.
- Your Replit preview URL and Replit username.
- A description of how Replit helped you move from idea to income.
