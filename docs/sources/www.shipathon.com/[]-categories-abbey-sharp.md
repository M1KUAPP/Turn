## The nutrition && healthy eating challenge

Abbey's audience is made up largely of millennial women who have spent years
struggling with their relationship with food. They have real health goals, but
they do not want another strict meal plan that falls apart at a restaurant, on a
trip, while feeding kids, or when eating leftovers at the sink.

Build a flexible app inspired by Abbey's **Hunger Crushing Combo** framework:
instead of asking people to restrict or remove food, help them answer, “What can
we add to make this meal more satisfying?”

The experience should support real women living real lives. It must not rely on
calorie counting or macro tracking.

## What judges are looking for

Judges are looking for a thoughtful solution that:

- Makes satisfying, balanced eating feel flexible rather than prescriptive.
- Works across messy, everyday situations instead of assuming an ideal
  meal-planning routine.
- Uses an additive approach rooted in the Hunger Crushing Combo framework.
- Avoids calorie counting, macro tracking, and diet-culture patterns.
- Feels compassionate, practical, and easy to return to.

## About Abbey Sharp

Abbey Sharp is a Registered Dietitian, bestselling author, award-winning content
creator, and a leading voice in evidence-based nutrition communication. Through
YouTube, social media, and her podcast, _Bite Back with Abbey Sharp_, she helps
millions cut through diet-culture noise and build a healthier, more peaceful
relationship with food. She is the author of _The Mindful Glow Cookbook_ and
_The Hunger Crushing Combo Method_ and is known for combining scientific rigor,
compassion, and sharp wit.

## Abbey's audience

Abbey's typical audience member is a millennial mom with health or weight-loss
goals who feels overwhelmed by competing nutrition messages and burnt out from
trying to micromanage her body and food. Her audience is 85% female and 15%
male, with the largest age groups being 35-44 (35%) and 25-34 (27%).

Her top audience countries, in order, are the United States, Canada, the United
Kingdom, and Australia. The reported age breakdown is 18-24 (13%), 25-34 (27%),
35-44 (35%), 45-54 (10%), 55-64 (7%), and 65+ (3%).
