## Ship kit

**I still haven't received the Ship Kit. Where should I claim?**

It usually takes **3 to 5 business days** after you register on Devpost. Please
allow some time for processing. If you still have not received anything after
**7 days**, please contact us at
**[shipkit@revenuecat.com](mailto:shipkit@revenuecat.com)** or ask in the
[#shipkit-support channel in Discord](https://discord.gg/shipaton26) .

## Eligibility and participation

**I do not have a Google Play Developer account or an Apple Developer account.
Can I still receive support or submit in another way?**

Possibly. Active students aged 13 and older may be eligible for the Next Gen
Award, which does not require an app store submission or a paid developer
account. You must provide a verifiable academic email address from a high
school, college, university, bootcamp, or another academic program. If you are
under the age of majority where you live, a parent or legal guardian must agree
to the official rules on your behalf. Review the
[Next Gen eligibility requirements](https://www.shipaton.com/next-gen) .

To participate in Shipaton outside of the Next Gen Award, you must be able to
integrate the RevenueCat SDK into your project, which includes setting up
products in the relevant app stores.

**I am under the age of majority where I live. Can I still participate?**

Yes. Active students who are at least 13 can enter the Next Gen Award. If you
are under the age of majority where you live, a parent or legal guardian must
review and agree to the official rules on your behalf and
[complete the guardian consent form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe07xUpIPsdr2FawYnwNeW096BkHV5_lnDpmPUy8VK0UW70IA/viewform)
. Signed written consent will be required before you can be announced as a
winner.

Minor entrants, and teams that include a minor entrant, may compete only for the
Next Gen Award. Review the
[Next Gen eligibility requirements](https://www.shipaton.com/next-gen) and the
[official rules](https://www.shipaton.com/go/devpost?to=%2Frules) before
entering.

**Can participants apply for both the Next Gen Award and another category at the
same time?**

It depends on the entrant's age. Students who have reached the age of majority
where they live may qualify for the Next Gen Award and another category if they
meet both categories' requirements.

Minor entrants, and teams that include a minor entrant, may compete only for the
Next Gen Award. For Next Gen judging, a store submission is allowed but is not
considered as part of the judging criteria.

**Is there any limit to the number of teammates?**

No. Though we'll only fly one of you to NY when you win an eligible prize.

**What countries are eligible? Are residents from Brazil or Quebec allowed to
participate?**

Depending on where you reside, United States law or your local laws may prohibit
you from participating in a competition or receiving a prize. Because hackathons
are considered competitions, Shipaton must follow these requirements.

New this year, **individuals residing in Quebec or Brazil ARE allowed to
participate** in the Shipaton.

Individuals who reside in the following countries and regions may be excluded
from participation or prize eligibility:

Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Crimea, and Russia.

## Schedule

**When is the deadline?**

Shipaton will run from **August 1 to September 30, 2026**.

The final submission deadline is **Wednesday, September 30, 2026 at 11:45 PM
Pacific Time**, so the exact deadline may vary slightly depending on your
country or time zone. Please make sure to check the deadline carefully based on
your local time.

**When will the winners be announced?**

Winners will be announced on **October 22, 2026**.

## Prizes

Devpost only shows the prize money we have assigned to specific categories so
far. We have secured over **$700,000 in cash prizes**, but we are still
finalizing some prize categories, so Devpost will not show the full amount yet.

By the start of the event on **August 1, 2026**, you will see the full prize
amount reflected on Devpost.

## Submissions

**What platforms are allowed for Shipaton submissions?**

Shipaton submissions may be iOS, macOS, or Android apps.

To qualify, your app must be submitted to one of the supported app stores:
Apple's App Store, Google Play Store, or Samsung Galaxy Store.

**Do I need to use the RevenueCat SDK when building my app?**

Yes. All Shipaton submissions must use the RevenueCat SDK for in-app purchases,
subscriptions, or ads.

**Can I submit multiple apps?**

Yes. However, to qualify, your app must be shipped for the first time during the
Shipaton window, from August 1 to September 30.

You can start building or even promoting your app before then, but you should
not publish it to the app stores before August 1 if you want it to remain
eligible.

**Can I submit an app that is already live on one platform but not yet shipped
on another?**

No. To qualify for Shipaton, the app itself must be shipped for the first time
during the Shipaton window. Launching it on a second store does not count if it
was already live in another app store.

For example, if your app was live on Google Play before August 1, then
submitting the same app to the App Store after August 1 would not qualify.

The exception is web apps. If your project only existed on the web before
Shipaton, you may submit a new app-store version as long as its first store
release happens during the event window.

**My app is still under review on the store. Can I still win?**

No. Your app must be published on the app stores so judges can download and
review it.

App review usually takes a few business days, assuming your app is not rejected.
We recommend submitting your app to the app stores at least one week before the
Shipaton deadline and getting ready to get scored.

## Prizes and winners

**Do winners need to attend the RAGA event in NYC to receive the prize? What
happens if the grand prize winner doesn't have a visa?**

No. If you win, the prize money will be sent directly to your bank account.
Attending the NYC RAGA event in person is not required. If you would like to
attend, we'll help however we can with the visa process so you can receive it
quickly.

If you are unable to attend, you may send someone on your behalf. You can also
receive the award and prize fully remotely.
