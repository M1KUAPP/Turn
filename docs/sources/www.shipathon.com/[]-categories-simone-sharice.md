## The yoga && fitness challenge

Simone's audience includes women in their late 20s through early 50s:
professionals, entrepreneurs, caregivers, and moms. Wellness has become
overwhelming for them. They do not need more advice; they need help deciding
what to do today.

Build a personalized daily wellness companion that answers that question. It
might bring together Pilates classes, recovery, and other parts of a person's
journey into one practical plan that makes wellness decisions easier and more
livable.

## What judges are looking for

Judges are looking for a solution that:

- Turns a person's wellness journey into a clear, useful plan for today.
- Personalizes recommendations without creating more decisions or information
  overload.
- Balances movement, classes, recovery, and the realities of a busy life.
- Feels supportive and achievable for women with different responsibilities and
  routines.
- Helps people act consistently instead of simply giving them more advice.

## About Simone Sharice

Simone Sharice is an Atlanta-based lifestyle, beauty, and fitness creator,
certified Pilates instructor, and founder of @sundaiolivepilates. She began
creating YouTube tutorials that made DIY protective hairstyles accessible and
has since expanded into wellness, fitness, and everyday lifestyle inspiration.
Her work blends education, empowerment, and personal style to encourage
self-care, confidence, and holistic wellness.

## Simone's audience

Simone's typical audience member is a woman who prioritizes her health and wants
trusted advice, inspiration, and practical tips on wellness, beauty, and
balanced living. Her audience is 94.8% female and 5.2% male, with the largest
age groups being 35-44 (37.2%) and 25-34 (33.6%).

Her top audience countries are the United States (76.6%), the United Kingdom
(3.1%), Canada (2.7%), and South Africa (2.3%). The full age breakdown is 13-17
(0.1%), 18-24 (2.9%), 25-34 (33.6%), 35-44 (37.2%), 45-54 (18%), 55-64 (6%), and
65+ (2%).
