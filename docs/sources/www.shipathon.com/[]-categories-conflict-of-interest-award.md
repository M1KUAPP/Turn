## What judges are looking for

The Conflict of Interest Award is a special non-cash category for eligible
submissions from RevenueCat or sponsor employees. It gives builders connected to
the event a place to participate without competing for the standard cash prizes.

## What to include

- A clear description of the app and what you built during Shipaton.
- A demo video showing the project working.
- Any context judges need to understand your connection to RevenueCat, sponsors,
  or the event.
- Source code, app links, screenshots, and testing access where applicable.
