## What judges are looking for

The RevenueCat Peace Prize recognizes the project with the strongest social
good. Judges are looking for apps that benefit individuals, communities, or
society at large.

Strong submissions explain who the app helps, why the problem matters, and how
the product design supports that impact.

## What to include

- A description of the audience or community the app serves.
- The problem the app is trying to solve and why it matters.
- How the app was designed to create positive impact.
- Any early evidence of usefulness, adoption, feedback, or real-world benefit.
