## What judges are looking for

The RevenueCat Design Award celebrates the craft of app development, separate
from business viability. Judges are looking for innovative ideas, beautiful
interface design, thoughtful interaction design, and animations that make the
app feel better to use.

This category is about taste, care, and execution.

## What to include

- A description of the app's unique design elements.
- Specific screens, flows, interactions, or animations judges should pay
  attention to.
- Notes on how design decisions support the product experience.
- A demo video that clearly shows the app in motion.
