campaigns using the API, MCP, or dashboard.

## Requirements

Your app must be live and include a working OneSignal integration. One
thoughtfully timed push notification can qualify, while a well-designed
multi-step Journey can make the submission stand out.

## What judges are looking for

- **Implementation:** Are the fundamental OneSignal features integrated cleanly,
  reliably, and successfully?
- **User value:** Does the integration improve the overall experience or add
  meaningful value?
- **Resourcefulness:** Did the team use OneSignal creatively or unexpectedly to
  achieve its intended outcome?
- **Depth:** Did the team explore less common or more advanced mobile messaging
  capabilities?

## What to include

- Your OneSignal App ID.
- A description of how you used OneSignal.
