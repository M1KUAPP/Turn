## Requirements

Your app must be live, and you must use Noise as part of your growth strategy.

## What judges are looking for

- **Virality:** Did your creative and user-generated-content playbooks on Noise
  produce one or more viral or semi-viral posts?
- **Scalability:** Are the creative formats easy to repeat and distribute at
  scale?
- **Conversion relevance:** Do the creatives clearly communicate the need for or
  novelty of the app, connect with a real user pain point, and position the
  product as the solution?

## What to include

- A live app URL.
- The email address associated with your Noise account.
