## Failures

- **Teammate reads that didn't happen: CONTENT-1, CONTENT-3, and CONTENT-4.** Their checks ask a teammate to read the starter phrases, to compare both versions of the consent texts with the requirements, and to compare the privacy notice with the TRD's data inventory. #75 and #76 closed as not planned. Tests check what a script can: `packages/eval/test/starter-bank.test.ts`, `apps/mobile/test/consent-strings.test.ts`, and `apps/mobile/test/privacy-notice.test.ts`.
- **One slow cold launch: PERF-3 and BANK-7.** One of ten launches at 2,156 phrases took 2.11 s, loading its JavaScript from Metro.
- **One flow still fails at AX5 on v2: `consent-under18`.** On `e3e7811`, after its tap the under-18 switch can't be found again or doesn't read as on; it passes at the default size, and CONSENT-6 keeps the pass recorded on an earlier build. `paywall-buy`, `paywall-failed`, and `erase-purchase` failed at AX5 too, since RevenueCat's published v2 paywall put its Unlock Listen mode button below the fold; #185 scrolls to it, and reports `paywall-buy` and `paywall-failed` passing at both sizes.
- **No Personal Voice on either iPhone checked: VOICE-2 and COMPAT-3.** iOS takes hours to make one, so only Turn's note and the system voice were seen; #180 tracks the playback and the deny path.
